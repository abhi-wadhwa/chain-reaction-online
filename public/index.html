How to play
  1. Place an orb in an empty cell or one of your own.
  2. Cells burst at 2 orbs in corners, 3 on edges, 4 inside.
  3. Each burst sends an orb to every adjacent cell, turning those cells your color.
  4. After both players have moved, capture all your opponent’s orbs to win.

Coral goes first. Keep both tabs open. Rooms sync through the server. Refreshing restores your seat in this tab. Rooms expire after two hours.
